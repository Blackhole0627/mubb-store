7. Purchase flow and emails

Catalog → Product → Add to cart → Cart → Checkout → Gateway (Datafast / Kushki) → Result page

email 1Order placed (customer + MUBB)   email 2Payment confirmed   email 3Status changed: shipped / delivered

stockHeld at checkout start (30 min) → decremented when paid → released if rejected or expired → low-stock alert email to MUBB
Admin panel (not drawn here, standard CMS screens): Products and variants, stock, photos, categories · Orders list with filters, status change, customer and payment details, CSV export · Shipping zones and prices · Showroom pickup points · Policy pages.
